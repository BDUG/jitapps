How to use sheetiX ↗
A1

A little formula fluency.

Start with =. Reference cells, ranges and other sheets.

=A1 * B1
=SUM(C2:C20)
=AVERAGE(B1:B10)
=IF(A1 > 100, "Above", "Below")
=ROUND(A1 / 3, 2)
='Sheet 2'!A1 + Sheet1!B2

Browse the searchable function catalog below for supported functions and examples. Percentages and comma or semicolon argument separators are supported. Operators: + − * / ^ &, =, <>, <, >, <=, >=. Parentheses and quoted text are supported. Cycles and unsupported formulas show errors.

Click to select; Shift + click selects a range. Double-click, F2, or use the formula bar to edit. Copy and paste rectangular blocks, including tabs/newlines from other spreadsheets. Formulas pasted as text retain their references.
